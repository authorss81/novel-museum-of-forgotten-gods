# Continuity State

Bootstrap canon is established. No chapter prose has been drafted; Volume 1 begins from its opening state in the bible and series outline.

## Canon locks

- The First Witness is a civic intelligence assembled from one chosen memory offered by each of 312 volunteers. Names and repeated acts were recorded as identifiers and anchors, not as alternative non-memory offerings.
- Volume 11 reveals only that the First Witness is a civic intelligence rather than an original god. Volume 12 fully explains its 312 memories, Tomas's counter-witness, and Elias's ability.
- The name lock, witness lock, and return lock are three functions. The central lock is the separate keeper core beneath the Museum; the final lock is the chamber's last automatic safety interlock, which cannot release automatically and requires a deliberate opening after a tested citywide custodian network can receive the First Witness's burden. The coalition finds the final lock in Volume 11, breaks the central lock in Volume 12, and opens the final lock in Volume 14.
- Orrin is detained after the Volume 12 chamber breach, released under a surviving Accord emergency order during the Volume 13 transfer, and returns to the ward-lines for the final conflict.
- Volume 13 causes only a temporary, uneven loss of Elias's object-hearing. The irreversible loss of most of that ability belongs to the Volume 14 climax.
- The Volume 11 cost is a permanent inability to re-read one specific father memory; the Volume 14 cost is the separate loss of Tomas's clear voice when his final echo is released. They are cumulative, not the same event.
- Open Hand is the final civic practice produced by Commons, not an additional personal stage.
- In the final charter, **tended** means an active relationship remains under Museum shared-trust custody, **returned** means its custody and interpretive authority have transferred to external living stewards, and **left absent** means no living community accepts an active relationship. These are disposition statuses, not synonyms for a successful reading.
