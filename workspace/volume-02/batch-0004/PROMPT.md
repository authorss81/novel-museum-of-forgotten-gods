## The voice, from the eighty drafted chapters

Third person, past tense, close on Elias. Plain declaratives with a long sentence every so often and a very short one at the beat. No purple, no fantasy diction, no invented technical language. **Elias thinks in doors, clocks, exits, stains on floors and what can be written down, and he has stopped counting objects and started counting rooms, and in this batch he has stopped collecting, has said his own rule out loud to a stranger once, has been asked a question by a professional for the first time in twelve weeks, and has been told that the thing he is proudest of is a hole in the case with a person standing in it.** When he is afraid he counts things and is embarrassed by the counting. **He is not a night guard. He is a man of thirty-two with no post, no access, no employer, no card, a pound spent on a bus fare, nine pages, a plastic wallet whose fourth item is worth nothing and is kept, and a cardboard folder, and that is not a temporary condition and it must not be written as one. He is on a municipal payroll at cost centre M.Q.S. 4 for a post that no longer exists, and no chapter may state or imply that he is on no list — and in this batch it should be said out loud, in a room, by somebody competent, that the payroll is the only thing keeping him a legal neighbour and that it is a payee field.** The category of person who *is* on no payroll and no rota and appears on no return of this building's is the **cleaning contract**, and that is Perry Loach.

Mara writes the same four lines about a thing she would write about a door and hates herself a bit afterwards. **She is procedural, not dismissive, she is right, and she has said *I do not know what you are for* and has not unsaid it.** **Ivo jokes when he is frightened and builds things with his hands and is never a mystic, and his condition from 1 December that every photographic exposure of collections material by a member of staff must be countersigned at the point of making is in force and it bites.** Hattie Bramm is a queue, a folder, a hard hand and thirty-one years, and **the hundred and forty are in a drawer and she will not be asked an eighth time by a Museum.** Doreen Sarn keeps her own counsel and her own copy and has refused seven things and none has moved, and she is not coming and is asked anyway. **Nobody in this batch is a villain and everybody in this batch is competent, including the records officer, the registrar, the letting agent, the solicitor, the trustee, the registration officer, the postman, the woman of eighty-one, and the assessor in Northglass who has now been spoken to and is not frightened of anybody.**
