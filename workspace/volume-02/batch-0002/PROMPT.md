## The handoff, as of the close of Chapter 60

**A name field is for the bill.** That is the answer, and it is in a box at the top of a form, and it has been there since about 2011, and nobody in this business has read it. ## The voice, from the sixty drafted chapters

No purple, no fantasy diction, no invented technical language. **Elias thinks in doors, clocks, exits, stains on floors and what can be written down, and he has stopped counting objects and started counting rooms and he noticed himself doing it in Chapter 52 and has not admitted it since.
