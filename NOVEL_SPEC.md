# Novel Specification

Title: The Museum of Forgotten Gods
Repository slug: novel-museum-of-forgotten-gods
Genre: urban fantasy / institutional horror
Male lead: A museum night guard who hears object memories
Length target: 720 chapters
Relationship policy: One slow-burn relationship or no romance, decided in the bible phase.
Power rule: Growth must be earned through practice, cost, failure, relationships, and changed decisions. The lead must not become instantly overpowered unless the approved genre premise requires it.

## Premise
A night guard hears memories in objects whose gods have been forgotten, and must decide what the objects still want.

## Ending
The museum chooses whether gods belong in exhibits, sanctuaries, or the lives of the living.
