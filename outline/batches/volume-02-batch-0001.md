## The batch as planned, in one paragraph

A name field empties across about four hundred and twelve addresses and the Museum loses the same rows at the same rate. The instrument is a rounds sheet, a field, a date, and a body pressing a key. A household object comes into the Museum on a form carried by a person who is not him, because a form is the only door in this city that has ever opened anything. And the batch ends on a question, not an event: **what is a name field for.**

## Chapter 0051 — The Fourth Day

**POV:** Elias Venn. **Where/when:** Tuesday 15 December 2024, a rented room above a laundrette in Low Ward, and a letting agency on Sillick Lane.

**Goal:** Get through a Tuesday with no income, no instrument and no standing in any room in this city.

**Resistance:** A letting agency and a woman with a book. The reference he can produce says nothing. He cannot go to the Museum and is barred from it from 1 December as a security matter, save attendance at a hearing or inquiry at which he is a party or a witness, on notice, in company, not before eight.

**Delivers:** the post from Mara Iles and the fact that the Director opened a door four miles away on Sunday morning while he stood on the pavement; the first four days of a life with no post; and then **he goes to number 88 and knocks again, and this time the person has a document, and the document is the wrong document.** A bank statement with his name on it four times and the right address on it, posted on the second, money taken on the second, returned on the eighth with a dash in the box where the name goes. **The wrongness is structural: a piece of paper that proves he has money and not that he lives anywhere.**

**Ending:** the name field at 88 has been empty since 6 December and the person answering the door cannot produce one document with his own name on it. *I've never had a letter. I've had a bin bag and a bank.*

**Divergence:** the card said the chapter would be told "through a letting agency on Sillick Lane and a woman with a book." It is, and the chapter's real subject turned out to be the wrong document rather than the reference.
