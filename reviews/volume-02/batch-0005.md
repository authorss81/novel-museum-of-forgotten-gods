## The two repairs that changed the writing

### Finding 5 — the fifth line, and it was the chapter that was right

Chapter 92 has Mara Iles say, in front of the room, that the sheet's last line says nobody signs it, that she is asking them to sign it anyway, that she is not taking the line off, and that **if they sign it the last line is wrong and it will stay wrong on the paper.** Chapter 94 then said the line *is not on either of them* and that it *has been completely true for twenty-three weeks*, and Chapter 100 said it *has been completely true for every day of the twenty-five weeks in which it has existed*. The fifth line was drafted on 24 April; the maximum elapsed at those two points is 22 days and 37 days.

**The review was right that the chapters contradicted each other and wrong about which one to believe. The correct reading is better than the one that was on the page: the fifth line is true of the original and of everything anybody has done on that street, and it is now wrong on one sheet on purpose, and nobody is going to take the line off there either.** Both chapters now say that, and Ch 94's "completely true for twenty-three weeks" and Ch 100's "every day of the twenty-five weeks in which it has existed" are gone with the false arithmetic. **A boundary that says nobody signs it, with three signatures on it, kept wrong on purpose by a registrar who refused to erase it, is the volume's thesis. The version that was on the page had thrown it away.**

### Finding 32 — a heading that promised a scene the chapter did not write, and the chapter it should have written instead

Chapter 92's heading read *And two more signed and two said no, and nobody wrote down which was which*. **The section showed neither. It cut straight to Frank Dunning.** The notebook then reported three names, and nine people had been asked, and 3 + 2 = 5 of 9 were unaccounted for and nobody said so.

**The heading now says what the section does, and the section does it.** Somebody counts the names in the wallet. Somebody else does the sum out loud: three of nine signed, one refusal was spoken and not written, **four of nine accounted for and five of them are not**, and the registrar says in about nine seconds that she is not going to account for the five, **because either she would be writing them down or she would be asking people in front of each other, and that is the same instrument with the manners taken off** — and then the half that matters: three out of nine is not a finding about this street, nobody is going to stand in a hall and tell nine people what a third of them did with a pen, there is no column for it, and the day somebody draws it is the day this is a programme. **The batch's argument, turned on its own arithmetic, in the room where the arithmetic was done. This is better than the heading it replaced and it is the reason the chapter holds.**
