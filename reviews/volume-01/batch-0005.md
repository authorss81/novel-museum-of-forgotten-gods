## The two structural rulings

### Ruling 1 — the door notice (Chapters 49 and 50)

Chapter 49 spends its last four paragraphs on the fact that Elias Venn is not told: *He has not been told. He is not on the distribution… he will read it on Monday, and Monday is not this.* Chapter 50 had him receiving the note through a Low Ward letter box at about half past two on the Friday afternoon and acting on it. A letter posted at four o'clock on Saturday cannot be in a box at half past two on Friday. **`workspace/volume-02/batch-0001/PROMPT.md` had adopted Chapter 50's version, so Volume 02 inherited the contradiction.**

**Chapter 50 has been rewritten at the head and nothing else in it moved.** He is on the pavement because **he is barred** — a piece of paper in a plastic sleeve in his coat, dated 25 November, saying barred as from 1 December as a security matter, with no finding and nothing to appeal — and because **the reopening date came off a printed card in a newspaper** and nobody has told him anything. The note is still posted on Saturday 12 December at about four o'clock and is still read on Tuesday 15 December. The fixed final image is intact and is now, in addition, actually complete: the closing clause — *a man who can only guard buildings has been told, in writing, in a plastic sleeve, that he is not a building* — is rendered, which the review noted it never was.

### Ruling 2 — the sixteenth-day closure, and the December calendar

`outline/volume-01.md` says *the Hall closes for a month*. The Batch 0005 prompt says the Director opens the door *one month after the memorial*. The same prompt pins Chapter 50 to Saturday 13 December. **A month after Friday 27 November is 27 December, and 13 December is sixteen days.** The prompt's own day map is also internally inconsistent — it puts 30 November at a Monday and 12 December at a Friday, and no week can produce both — and the draft had followed the December half, which put **every December date in the batch one day out**.

**The pinned November line governs and it governs December: `Sun 15 … Sat 28 November`, therefore `Sun 29 · Mon 30 Nov · Tue 1 … Sat 12 · Sun 13 December` and `Thu 17 December`. 1 December 2024 is a Tuesday. 13 December 2024 is a Sunday.** Every December weekday in Chapters 47–50 has been re-derived and re-grepped.

**The closure is sixteen days**, the Board's minute sets 13 December, the Director moves nothing because a condition is a condition, and no chapter calls it a month. Recorded as `state/continuity.md` R2. The alternative — moving the reopening to 27 December — would have cost the volume its final image, its last door, the close of the count and Volume 02's first page. That is not a repair; it is a rewrite.
