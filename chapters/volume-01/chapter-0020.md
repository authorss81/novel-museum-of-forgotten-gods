# Chapter 0020 — Thirty-Eight Children and Two Teachers

This one did not ask for his own account.

Elias Venn read it standing up and then went and got the day book and turned back to the Sunday and wrote under Sunday the eighteenth, in the blank half of the page, in pencil, in his own hand, one line, which was a notice and not an accusation and had his name and initials against it.
