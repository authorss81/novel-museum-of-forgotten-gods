# Chapter 0156 — Legally Uncountable

## And on the Monday morning a curator opened a room about nine metres from the west stair and there were about nine objects on about nine trays in it and the trays are not the collections trays

The room is at the west end of that corridor and it is entered from the corridor about nine metres short of the stair, and it is about four metres by three, and it has no window, and it is warmer than the corridor because the hot water runs through the wall on that side, and about nine people come there a week in an ordinary month and sit, and the reason they sit in there and not in the corridor is that it is warm and that the door shuts.

**There are two kinds of tray in the conservation store on that floor and everybody in the building knows the difference and neither kind is in the schedule of collections.** The collections trays are grey and they have a slip of card in them with a number on it, and the number is an accession reference, and the year in the reference is the year the series was opened and not the year the object came in.

The other kind are brown, and there are about nine of them, and a brown tray has a custody label on it and a custody label is not a card and it has no number on it and it is not in any index and there is a book on the second level in which every custody label that has ever been printed is copied out by hand, and the book is a hardback with a quarter binding and it is about two hundred pages and it is not a Museum record and it is a book that a person keeps.

"**Say what the label says.**"

"**It says three things and a place,**" said Menna Prothero. "**What the object is, what it is made of, what state it is in, and where it is. That is all. It is a label for a thing, and a thing is what a label is for, and I did not invent it and I have not improved on it in nineteen years.**"

"**Say what it does not say.**"

"**It does not say who brought it and it does not say when, and it does not say a name, and there is no box for any of that and there has never been a box for any of that on any form in this building,**" she said, "**and a woman in the label room who has been doing the job longer than I have said that not one card in this building has got a person on it, and she is right, and it is a hundred and twenty years of practice and I did not start it and I am not going to stop it.**"

"**Then how do you know whose they are.**"

"**I do not, and I have never asked, and asking is the whole of the rule.**" She put her hand on the nearest brown tray. "**The rule is mine and it is four lines long and I wrote it in about 1998 and it has never been printed and it has never been in a minute. A person may bring a thing up to this floor. It will be put on a tray and it will be labelled and it will be looked after and it will be here when they come back. And nobody will write their name on anything, and nobody will ask them where they live, and nobody will ask them what the thing is for, and if anybody in this building asks any of those three questions in front of the person then I have failed at something and I will know about it.**"

"**Why.**"

"**Because those are the three questions,**" said Menna Prothero, "**and there is a reason for each one and the reason for the first one is that a name is a hook, and the reason for the second one is an address, and the reason for the third one is a purpose, and this case has spent every week since the first of June finding out that all three of those are instruments, and I am not a person who is going to hold one on behalf of somebody who came up here on a Thursday.**"

"**And the trays.**"

"**And the trays are not accessioned and they are not on the schedule and they are not in the archive schedule and they have no work order and no `PF` number and no `PF` file and no cost centre, and there are about nine of them and there have been about nine of them for as long as I have been here, and about nine people come up to this floor, and I have never once written down how many, and I have been asked about it about four times and I have said about nine every time and about nine is not a number I keep.**"

## And on the Monday afternoon a registrar of twenty-eight explained the municipal condition in four sentences in a records office, and she did not say the instrument was wrong, and nobody asked her to, and a curator named the thing

The room was on the Monday and the explanation was in a records office on the ground floor at about half past two with four people in it, and it was four sentences and Mara Iles gave it standing up, with a file open on a table, and the file had four lines in it on a single side.

"**A housing officer with a statutory power signed an instrument on Friday the fifth of November 2025 that sealed the legal description of a registration district. "**And on the twelfth of November a district register at Northglass wrote four lines in answer to a question this case asked, and the four lines say that the register does not hold a registration district of the name Ferry Bank, that it holds descriptions, boundaries and occupiers and does not make instruments and does not sign them, that the premises to which the enquiry relates are not within a registration district and the register has no record of them at that name, and that no further action is available to that office in the matter.**"

"**About nineteen hundred tenancies,**" said Mara Iles, "**and I am not going to say a second number, and I am going to be asked to, and the answer is going to be the same every time for the rest of this case: no register, office, register of electors, rent roll, water account, electoral roll, hospital record or school roll in this county can say how many people live behind about nineteen hundred tenancies, and about nine hundred people is a figure that was used in November about a claim queue and it is a figure about a queue and not about a district, and if anybody in this case writes a population for that district then I will know and I will not be quiet about it.**"

"**There is no figure for the people and there never will be one and the only figure anybody in this case will ever have about them is the number who came up on a given day, and on an ordinary day that is about nine, and about nine is a number of a morning and not a number of anything else, and I have said that sentence out loud twice in twenty minutes and I am saying it a third time now because there are four of you in this room and I would like all four of you to have it.**"

"**Say the word for it.**"

"**Say the word for what.**"

"**Legally uncountable. I did not coin it. "**And it is not a curse and it is not a haunting and it is not a punishment,**" she said. "**It is a condition. "**Then what connects them.**"

"**Nothing connects them,**" said Menna Prothero. "**That is the sentence and I have not said it in nineteen years and I have said it today, and about nine people come up to that floor and about nine of them cannot be produced from any file in this city and one of them is at an address in a district a register wrote four lines about in November, and I have told you the singular because the singular is what I know and the singular is not a count.
