# Chapter 0154 — A Cartridge Is A Schedule

**On Monday the thirteenth and Tuesday the fourteenth of December 2025 a positive-pressure breathing mask came out of a box in a conservation store on the fourth level of a building on Civic Hill, and it was held for about ninety seconds by a man of thirty-two who is the only person in this case who can hold a thing, and what came back was nothing at all, and four people were in the room and none of them knows what he is, and nobody was frightened, and the chapter does not improve on it and does not make it longer than four sentences.**

**Rubber, a moulded facepiece, a head harness, an inlet boss, and a filter cartridge. It came to this building in 2014 in a bequest with a clause in it about the donor being told what was happening to it, and the donor is a woman of seventy-four called Rosalind Peake who is alive, and she is one of the people that floor is for, and she does not know it, and nobody in this case is going to ring her.**

**No card is cut for it in this chapter. There is a card for the object and there was one in 2014 and there is not going to be another one, and the reason is not hers and the reason is a form, and a form is the whole of this volume.**

## And on the Monday morning a curator of fifty-two did a condition check on an object with a form, and the year in the number was not the year it came in, and a woman in the label room said a sentence in February that nobody improved on and nobody has improved on since

The conservation store on the fourth level is a room with a bench in it and a run of flat-fronted cupboards and a rack of about nine boxes on the top shelf that are the oldest stock in the building and are not the Hall of Former Faiths and have never been anything to do with the Hall of Former Faiths. **The grid has a column for condition, and a column for the date of the check, and a column for the initials of the person who did it, and it has no column for what the object is, and it has no column for who it belongs to, and it has no column for where it has been, and Menna Prothero has been filling that grid in for nineteen years and has about nine hundred sheets of it in four cupboards and it is the largest body of information about the objects on that floor that exists in this city.**

"**What is this one.**" said Elias Venn.

"**That one is a breathing mask and it has been in that box since the eleventh of September 2014 and I have checked it every year since and this is the eleventh, and I do that because the form says to do it and because the bequest says to do it, and those are two different reasons and one of them is enforceable.**" She put the box on the bench. "**It is not on display and it is never going to be on display and that is a condition of the bequest and I have never tested that condition and I am not going to.**"

"**Say the number.**"

"**`1968/BQ/22`.**"

"**That is not 2014.**"

"**No.**"

"**The accession rule is that the year is the year the series was opened and not the year the object came in,**" said Rhiannon Iddon, from the doorway, with nineteen years of it in her voice. "**`BQ` is Bequests, general, and the series was opened in 1968 out of the Kettering bequest, and every object in it carries 1968 whatever year it came through the door. "**And people get that wrong.**"

"**About four times in nineteen years and every one of the four was somebody who had not read page two, and page two is where the year is, and I have stopped explaining it.**"

"**Say the other thing,**" said Menna Prothero, "**which is that the form does not have a column for the year it came in either, and that is not an oversight, and I have asked for a column for that in about 1998 and about 2006 and about 2019 and there is a person in this city whose job it is to say no to that, and the answer is always the same, and the answer is correct.**"

"**That the accession number is what the object *is* and the date of arrival is a fact about a movement between two buildings, and movements are not part of what a thing is.**" She turned the box about ninety degrees so the label faced up. "**I have never agreed with that and I have never needed to and I am not going to start now, because there is a bequest file in this building with a date in it and a donor in it and a clause in it, and I do not need a column, and neither does anybody who has ever been in that room.**"

And then the label room, which is windowless and is on the second level, sent a sentence down ninety metres of building in the form of a woman of fifty-five or sixty who had been doing the job a very long time and who had a surname on a badge, and what came down it was a thing Elias Venn had heard once, in a conservation office, on a Thursday morning in February 2025, and which nobody has improved on since and which nobody in this building is going to improve on now.

"**Fenn says that you can card a bell and you cannot card a rope,**" said Menna Prothero, "**and that there is no line on the form for it and she has looked twice. She has also said it about a key. "**Say what she meant,**" said Rhiannon Iddon.

"**She meant that the day somebody prints a card that says what a thing is for, that thing is an object, and it goes in a case, and then it is furniture with an accession number, and she has seen a bin of nine in the store and has never once wanted a number for one of them.**"

## And the mask is rubber, and a moulded facepiece, and a head harness, and an inlet boss, and a cartridge, and it is the cartridge that is the object and nobody in the room has ever said so

She took it out at about eleven o'clock on the Monday morning and put it on the grey mat and it took her about nine seconds to do it and he watched all nine.

It is not a mask in the sense of a thing a person puts on their face. ## And a man of thirty-two held it for about ninety seconds, and what came back was nothing at all, and he was not frightened, and the chapter is four sentences long and it is not a scene
