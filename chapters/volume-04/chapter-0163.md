# Chapter 0163 — What A Renewal Is A Record Of

**On Thursday the thirteenth and Friday the fourteenth of January 2026 a bag of about forty used filter cartridges came off a shelf in a windowless room on the second level of a building on a hill, a shelf that is not on the schedule, and four of them were already in a coat pocket in the hands of a man of thirty-two who had been given them on purpose on the third of January by a curator of fifty-two who said *do not write my name on the bag*.**

**A used cartridge is a record of hours. A cartridge renewed on a date is a record of when somebody was not in a room. A woman of fifty-two said that in a records office on the fourteenth of December 2025 and nobody has improved on it since. On Thursday a woman in a label room with a surname on a badge said the other half of it, which nobody had asked her for, and the other half is that the mask is on a shelf and a curator fits a cartridge to it about four times a year to keep it serviceable, and a cartridge that is fitted and taken off is a record of a person at a bench and not of hours at all, and the hours in it are the hours of a store room, and the renewal is a record of when the mask was not in a room, which is always.**

**And nobody asked a woman of seventy-four any of it. ## And a man of thirty-two put four dates in a list he cannot check on the table, and the woman in the label room asked him to write them, and the reason she asked him is the reason nobody else in that room would

He put four cartridges on the cutting board and a notebook beside them and wrote the four dates down, and he could not tell whether he had written them in the order they came out of the bag, and there is no form for the order of a list, and he did not say so.
