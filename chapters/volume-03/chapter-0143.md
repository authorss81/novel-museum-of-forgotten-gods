## And the room that did the planning had one picture in it and it is a photograph of a rope on a wet table taken in June by a person with a telephone, and nobody has looked at it since June, and the plan is built on it

## And on the Saturday afternoon at about four o'clock there were about nine people in a laundrette on the low bank with the machines going, and the rota on the back of the counter has a name against Monday the twenty-fifth, and nobody said what the week is for
