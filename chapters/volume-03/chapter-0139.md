**The order does not exist. ## And on the Thursday afternoon at about two o'clock a woman of fifty read a covering note out loud in a room on a hill and there were seven people in it, and one of them had asked her to come, and one of them had not been asked by anybody in this case at all

> **Lyle, Sadler & Barrow. Third floor, Lantern Row.**
> **7 October 2025.**
> **Our client has asked, in writing, whether a compensation roll for a registration district may be settled while the district's occupied frontages cannot be established. We have advised. > **1. The instrument will not say that any person is not present. Presence is not in issue. Any person at an address in the district continues to be described as present at that address for every purpose this office can think of, including rates, tenancy, policy, claim and register.**
> **2. The instrument will seal the legal description of the district and not the district. A tenancy, a rate, a policy, a claim, a register entry and a door do not depend on a description and will continue in force, and each of them will continue to produce correspondence, and every letter any of them produces will be correct.**
> **3. The reason is arithmetic and not administration. A compensation roll is a set that has to close. This district cannot close. Occupancy at a frontage is established from a tenancy. "**Say it.**

"**A person who is hard to find is a person whose existence somebody may deny. A person with no legal existence is a person whose existence nobody needs to deny.**"

"**Is that mine.**"

"**It is yours and you put it in a clause and I have said it in about nine rooms since the first of September and I did not have your clause on the seventh of August when I said it the first time,**" said Hazel Cray, "**which I am aware of, and which is the reason I have said it in a room and not in a book.**"

"**That is the second time in five months that a person in this flood has said a thing out loud that a professional had already written down months earlier, and I would like somebody to write down how that keeps happening, and I would like it written by somebody who is not me.**"
