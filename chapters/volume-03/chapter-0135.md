# Chapter 0135 — Who Was In The Room

**On Thursday the twenty-third and Friday the twenty-fourth of September 2025 two professionals went to about eleven doors on the low bank behind four hundred metres of wall and asked one question at each of them, and eleven households answered it, and the question was who was in the room, and every one of the eleven could say who and none of the eleven could say how high the water came, and that was not a failure of the asking, it was the answer, and it took two days and about nine hours.**

**Nobody in this chapter is a villain and nobody in it is a fool.
