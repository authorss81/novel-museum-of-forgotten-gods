# Chapter 0116 — A Table, Asked For Again, In The Wrong Mood

**On Friday the twenty-third and Saturday the twenty-fourth of July 2025 a river engineer of fifty-five was asked again, by a woman of forty-eight with a statutory power, in the wrong mood, thirty-eight days after he had said he would like to be asked again in about four weeks in a different mood, and he gave the four reasons he had for not giving the table away, and a fifth reason arrived that he had not used before and that was about himself, and he did not say which way he went, and the chapter does not decide it, and the reader is left holding an instrument that may or may not exist in about four hundred offices.**

She had a form, a period in it, a bank that had moved since the twenty-third of June, and about sixty ground floors behind her, and she came into a room at eleven o'clock on a Friday morning and did not sit down, and the man of thirty-two has written in his own notebook that he has been unable to work out which way it went for an hour in a car park and has got nowhere, and that he is not going to guess, because a guess in that book is a thing about him.**

## And he gave the four reasons he had for not giving it away, in the order he had given them before, and he had given them three times, and he said that out loud before he started

"**I have said these four things to about nine people since March,**" said Gwilym Petherick. "**I am going to say them in the same order, because the order is the argument, and if I shuffle them then I am making it up, and I am not going to make it up on a Friday morning in a car park.**"

"**We are on a Friday morning in a car park,**" said Elias Venn.

"**Then I am not going to make it up.**"

He put his hand on his inside pocket without taking anything out of it.

"**One. The second it is in a hand that is not mine it becomes a standard. Not a reference, not a conversion, not a note in a folder. "**Two. "**Three. He took his hand out of his pocket.

"**That is the four. ## And a fifth reason arrived that he had not used, and it took a moment, and it was about him and not about the table, and afterwards he said that it was the only true one he had
