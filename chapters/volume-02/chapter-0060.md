# Chapter 0060 — The Same Date, in Two Systems

It took him five days and it took him four attempts and the fourth one is the one that is on the page, and the reason it took four attempts is that the first three were him being pleased with himself, and he noticed, which is the only reason there was a fourth.

The finding is a fact on a document. It is not a shape and it is not a feeling and it is not a god. **It is that the same date is written in two systems in two buildings four miles apart, and one of those systems is the source of the other, and nobody in either building has ever been told that the second one exists.**

Saturday the fifth of December 2024 is in both of them. On the afternoon of the fifth, at twenty-two minutes past four, a body pressed a key on a handheld in a street in Low Ward. At about eleven minutes past four the following morning, in an office on Marsh Lane, a file went out.

**Attempt one, on the Monday evening, at a kitchen table in Low Ward, and it was wrong, and the way it was wrong is on the page because it is the mistake every person in this business would make.**

He had read that note on the Wednesday. He had read it eleven times. On the Monday evening he wrote a sentence about the sixth because the sixth was the day that had been in his head since Sunday the thirteenth of December, and the fifth was the day that was in the document.

**Attempt two was on the Tuesday, and it was wrong in the other direction, and it was worse, because it was clever.**

**Attempt three was on the Wednesday, and it was the right shape, and it fell over for a reason that took him a whole evening to accept.**

**That is the sentence that stopped him on the Wednesday evening and it is the sentence this whole business has been arriving at for six weeks, and it is this: the only instrument that has ever caught one in this business is a record with a name against it, and a record with a name against it is in a building, and he cannot get into the building, and the building's instrument is being overwritten every morning by a file from another building that nobody has ever told him about.**

He wrote that down, and it is the third line of the Wednesday entry, and then he did the thing he has done about nine hundred times in six weeks, which is to go to bed, and then he did the other thing, which is to telephone somebody at about ten past ten at night, which he had done once in eleven weeks, on the twelfth of December, to Mara Iles, on a pavement in Lantern Row, after he had posted the form.
