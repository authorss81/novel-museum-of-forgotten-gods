# Chapter 0083 — A Knock

The room was on the ground floor of a building in Morningside with a window that gave onto a yard and a table that had been a table in about 1978, and at about half past ten on the morning of Thursday the eighteenth of March 2025 there were nine people in it and a hearing that had been listed provisionally at the direction of an officer who had not been asked to list it by anybody, and the case was called by its number and the number is in a file in three buildings.

**Nobody in that room was frightened. That is the first thing to say about the eighteenth of March, and it is the reason the chapter is what it is.**

---

The officer was called **Marchant** and she was about fifty-six and she had been a conciliation officer for nineteen years and she had a printed list in front of her and a pen and no microphone, and she did the thing at the start that nobody expected, which was to read the Museum's own minute of the twenty-seventh of January out loud, in full, all four lines, including the two names, at a hearing to which the Museum had come as a party.

"**I am going to read this because this Museum made itself a party with four lines and two names and I am not going to let anybody in this room pretend it is a category of dispute, because it is not, it is a museum that has written itself into a matter on purpose and minuted that it would answer for being in it.**"

She read it. Nobody interrupted her. When she got to *A. WARD, Director* and *M. ILES, Senior Conservator and Accession Registrar* she did not slow down and she did not look up.

"**That is the basis of my jurisdiction in this matter and it is the only basis, and I want it on the record that it is the only basis, and that if the minute of the twenty-seventh of January were withdrawn this hearing would not be listed, and that is not a threat, it is a description of my desk.**"

"**Understood,**" said the partner.

"**Then let us find out what the matter is,**" said Marchant, "**and I am going to do it the way I do it, which is to ask each party in turn what they think the matter is about, and then to ask myself, out loud, in front of everybody, and then to write it down. The order matters. If I do it the other way round I will find the answer I have already got.**"

---

## The first answer came from a woman of about thirty-nine with a hardback book under her arm who had not been introduced and had not been asked to come

She had paid a pound, her name was in the visitors' register at a desk in a foyer, and she was a managing agent of about four hundred and twelve tenancies for one freeholder, and she had been in the room for eleven minutes before the officer asked her, and the reason she was there at all was that the hearing was listed on paper and paper goes to the managing agent's office.

"**I am not a party,**" said Sonia Boulton. "**My client has no position in this matter and I am here because a listing went out on paper and paper comes to me, and I would have been within my rights to send a manager and I have come myself, and I would like that in the minute, and I would also like it in the minute that I have not been asked to give evidence and I am not giving evidence. I am answering a question about a fact I manage.**"

"You may answer it," said Marchant.

"**Then the matter, as far as my client is concerned, is nine blocks and a cycle date.** About four hundred and twelve tenancies, a programme paper of the twenty-fourth of August 2023, quarterly, first day of each quarter, notices served on paper by hand at the property on the earliest date permitted by a person whose name is on the notice. Block seven went in on the first of April, which is nine days ago. Block eight goes in on the first of July. Block nine on the first of October, and after the first of October this programme is finished in this ward. **None of that is in dispute, I have said so in a public room, I have said so in a committee room, and I would like it said in this room in the same words, because a landlord has not done anything wrong and if people go away from today saying otherwise they will be wrong in a place where it matters.**"

"Nobody has alleged anything," said the associate.

"**Then I have nothing to do,**" said Sonia Boulton, "**and I would like to go and stand outside.**"

"Please sit down," said Marchant. "**I am going to ask the question that matters and it is not about the programme. It is: what is in front of me.**"

---

## The second answer was two words and they were the same two words as on the twenty-fourth of February, and nobody improved them

"**What does the Museum think the matter is about,**" said the partner. He was about fifty-two and he had a bundle and he had read the Museum's minute four times and he was not frightened of anybody in the room and did not perform it.

Mara Iles had her reading glasses off. She had not put them on for the officer's minute and she put them on for nothing.

"**We think the matter is about a knock.**"

The room did not do anything.

"**Say again,**" said the partner.

"**We think the matter is about a knock.**"

He wrote it down. He did not look up and he did not say anything and he did not say *I told you in February and you have said it again and I said I would not mention it*, and he did not need to, and the not-saying took about two seconds and everybody at that table including the officer understood exactly what had happened and exactly how much it had cost him to leave it alone.

"**Is that the whole of the Museum's position,**" he said.

"**That is the whole of the Museum's position and I am not going to add to it, and I would like it recorded that I have been asked twice in two rooms in four weeks to add to it and have not.**"

"**Why not.**"

"**Because it is the correct answer and it is short and every time I have made it longer I have said something about a person that did not need saying,**" said Mara Iles. "**I said the word *hook* in a public room in November and I said *a knock* in a committee room in February and I have said neither of them since, and if I say them again in front of nine people this afternoon then in about four years a person will read that this Museum's position at a hearing was a word, and it will be a word they can put in a sentence, and a word in a sentence in a hearing is the most dangerous object in this county.**"

---

## And then the officer asked herself the question out loud, in a room, which is not the normal way, and the answer took her a minute and a half

"**So. What is in front of me.**" Marchant put the pen down. "**I have a Museum that made itself a party in January and holds nothing and says so in four lines. I have a freeholder with no position and a managing agent who came instead of a manager. I have a solicitor for the freeholder who has asked me to hear what the Museum thinks the matter is about and has been given two words.**"

"**And I have a hearing. And a hearing is a thing I hold when two people disagree about something that can be put in a decision. So let me say what I think the disagreement is, and I am going to say it slowly, and I am going to say it in front of a witness, and I want it minuted exactly as I say it, and I do not want it tidied.**"

Nobody spoke.

"**The disagreement is not about a knock. There is no party in this room who objects to anybody knocking on anybody's door. If the matter were about knocking, I would have directed it to a different room in about nine seconds and we would all be somewhere else by lunchtime.**"

"**The matter in front of me is about about four hundred and twelve tenancies.** About four hundred and twelve households, in nine blocks, on a quarterly cycle, on a programme paper that any person may have at no charge on any working day of the week, and which nobody in this city had asked for in eighteen months until a Director of a museum asked a question in a letter and a letting agent came down a hill and read it out in a room and would not hand it over. That is lawful. Every step of it is lawful. Nobody has done anything wrong. **I have said that out loud now because a room full of people who have been in a case for thirteen weeks will take a lawful thing to be a cover for something else, and it is not, and if it is not then the thing that is actually wrong is somewhere else and it is not in front of me.**"

"Where is it," said the partner.

"**In a register that does not exist.**"

---

**And the officer said the sentence that reopens the whole business, and she said it to the table and not to the parties, and it took about forty seconds, and nobody in the room wrote it down except the clerk, and the clerk wrote all of it down.**

"**A tenancy in this country is a document that says a person is a neighbour of a place. It is signed, it is dated, it has a term on it, and it can be produced in a room by either side. It is the only piece of paper I know of that says a person belongs somewhere. Everything else we have is a name in a field, and a name in a field is a payee field, and a payee field is a bill, and a bill can be cleared overnight by a body at a door and nobody ever has to be asked.**"

"**So over about a fortnight in December, in one block of that programme, a number of households in this ward stopped being neighbours of anywhere, lawfully, correctly, on a cycle date, with a notice served by a person whose name was on it, and about nine of them have not been able to be found by a knock since, and not one of those nine was ever on any list anybody kept, because the person who knew them had a piece of paper in a drawer and would not give it to anybody, and the person who did not keep a list never had one.**"

"And what is your finding," said the associate.

"**It is not a finding. It is a note, and I am allowed to make a note, and I am going to say the rest of it as a note and I would like the word *forum* in it because I have spent nineteen years finding that word is the one nobody has.**"

Marchant wrote for about eleven seconds and then turned the sheet round and pushed it to the middle of the table, and it is in a file, and it is the first document anybody in this case has produced that is about the shape of the law rather than the content of a field, and here it is.

> **NOTE OF THE OFFICER, preliminary hearing, 18 March 2025.**
>
> **1. The practice referred to in the Museum's minute of 27 January 2025 is not the matter. No party objects to it. The matter is about about four hundred and twelve tenancies in nine blocks, a landlord's programme operating on quarterly cycle dates, and the loss, for a number of households, of any document that identifies a person as responsible for a place.**
>
> **2. There is no forum in this city in which a person may say that a landlord has taken the ability to be recognised as the neighbour of an address. The reason is not that such a forum has been refused, declined, or found wanting. The reason is that it has never been designed. No body in this city has a jurisdiction, a form, or an officer whose duty it is to hear a complaint about a person's recognisability as a neighbour.**
>
> **3. The instruments available to the parties in this matter are: a letting agent's statement of programme, which names no household; a Museum, which has four hundred forms and none of them reaches a door; a conciliation service, which has no jurisdiction over a landlord and no power over a delivery system; and a sheet of A4 in a plastic wallet on a table in a hall, which is not a party, is not represented, has no author, and is not producible by anybody.**
>
> **4. The only instrument that has been produced in this matter which has ever reached a household is a knock at a door and about nine names on a table. It is not a record. It is not admissible. It is the only thing anybody has.**
>
> **H. MARCHANT, conciliation officer.**

---

**Nobody spoke for about nine seconds and the partner asked the question that the officer had been waiting nineteen years for somebody competent to ask.**

"**Is that a finding that the service cannot hear this.**"

"**It is a note and it is the truth,**" said Marchant, "**and the answer is that this service can hear a dispute between two people about a tenancy, and it can hear a complaint about an administration, and it can hear about a delivery system, and there is nothing wrong with any of those. What this service cannot do is hear a person say *a landlord has taken the ability to be recognised as my neighbour*, because that is not a complaint about anybody's conduct. It is a description of a hole in the law, and I have no power to fill a hole in the law, and I would like it noted that a hole in the law is not the same as a failure of a person.**"

"Then what do you suggest," said Anja Ward, and she said it carefully, because she was the Director of the last institution in the chain and she had been in a room with a question like this before.

"**I suggest nothing. That is not modesty, Director, that is the job.**" Marchant squared the sheet. "**What I am going to do is give directions, and the directions are that the Museum produces nothing further, because it holds nothing; that the freeholder produces the programme, which it has already done in a document signed on the eleventh of February; and that the matter is listed again on a date to be set, and that the date is not today, and I am not going to give you one, because a date is the only thing in this matter anybody has wanted from me since the sixth of December and it is not mine to give and I have decided to stop pretending it is.**"

"And the note," said the associate. "Does the note go on a register?"

"**The note goes in the file. The file is not public. A determination is public and a note is not, and I have chosen a note on purpose, and I would like that choice noticed.**"

---

## And at about twenty past eleven the officer asked the man in the coat a question, and it was not on the list, and it is the only question anybody asked him in that room

He was there on a printed card with the word WITNESS in a box on it, in company, not before eight, and the systems technician of twenty-five was the company, and neither of them had spoken in fifty minutes.

"**You are the witness on the notice,**" said Marchant. "**I am going to ask you one question and it is a simple one and I would like a simple answer, and if the answer is *I do not know* then that is a complete answer and I will accept it in four seconds.**"

"Yes."

"**Is there a household in this matter.**"

Nobody in the room had asked that question before. It had been asked once, on the sixth of March, in a different building, forty miles away, by a statutory officer who had said she had noticed in February and had not mentioned it because she was busy, and nobody in this room knew that, and Marchant had arrived at it from a completely different direction and by a completely different road and in the same amount of time.

"**No,**" said Elias Venn.

"**Say a bit more than no, and then stop.**"

"**There is no household in this matter. There has not been one since the sixth of December. There are about nine people who have stood on a mat and said a name at a door and not one of them has been identified to you, or to me, or to this Museum, and there is no form on which any of them could be, and everybody in this room knows the reason and the reason is not a secret and it is on a piece of paper in a building on Marsh Lane that says *this file is not a register of persons*.**" He put his hands behind his back, which is what a person does at a table. "**And I have been asked a version of that question by two professionals in four weeks and I have been right both times and I have got nothing out of being right twice, and that is not a complaint and I am not asking anybody to do anything about it.**"

"**Thank you,**" said Marchant. "**That is the first time in nineteen years that a party has told me that the thing in front of me contains no people, and I am going to write it at the top of my own file and not in the note, because the note is about the law.**"

---

## And the last thing that happened in that room was a question about a chair, and nobody in the room could answer it

"**One more,**" said Marchant, looking at the list. "**The person who keeps the sheet. Is he here.**"

The room did not stop, because the room was already stopped from about ten past eleven and nothing since then had been fast.

"**No,**" said Mara Iles.

"**Was he invited.**"

"**He was not, and I want to be exact about it, because if I am not exact then it will be written down as an oversight of this Museum and it is not an oversight of this Museum, it is a decision of this Museum that I made in a corridor in February and defended twice.**"

"**Explain it, and do not defend it.**"

"**We wrote to him. He is a man of about sixty-five and he keeps the only record of this matter that exists and it is a sheet of A4 in a plastic wallet on a table in his own hall. My Director's minute of the twenty-seventh of January is four lines long and it says that this Museum will not receive, retain, request or file any record of the practice. That minute is the reason he is safe, and it is the reason he is not in this room, and it is the reason he was not asked whether he wanted to be in this room, and the reason he was not asked is that we decided in advance that he would say no and that the answer would be a no that we would then have to record or not record, and I was not prepared to put a man of sixty-five in a room with nine people and a solicitor and a question he had not been asked.**"

"That is the correct decision," said Marchant. "**And I want it noted, in the file and not in the note, that it is the correct decision and that it is the reason a matter which is about four hundred and twelve tenancies is being heard in a room where the only party with no landlord in it is a piece of A4 that is not in the room.**"

Nobody spoke.

"**And I would like to say one more thing and then I will take my directions and go and do my other work,**" said Marchant, "**and I have never said this in a hearing in nineteen years and I am saying it because there were nine people at that table on the eighteenth of March 2025 and nine of them did one thing today that was wrong, and not one of the nine did it on purpose, and every step of it was correct.**"

"**We did not ask him whether he wanted to be here. And that is how it happened. Not by a decision. By four competent people with a duty each, doing their duty correctly, on a form, in a corridor, on a Thursday.**" She picked her pen up. "**There is no form for that either. And I have checked, and I am the person who would know.**"
